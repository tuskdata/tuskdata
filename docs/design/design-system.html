The Tusk design system

A warm, editorial, calm system for a serious data product. Light-first. Built around Geist (UI), Geist Mono (data), and Instrument Serif (moments). Every value here is what ships in v3.

v0.3 Last updated · Nov 2025 · Owned by @joel
01 · Foundations

Principles

Five rules that should resolve 90% of design questions before you ask them.

1. Calm over clever.

Density matters but never overwhelms. Whitespace is structural. If a screen feels noisy, kill chrome before adding more.

2. Data is the hero.

Tables, queries, and numbers get our best typography (Geist Mono). UI chrome stays small and uncolored.

3. Warm, not corporate.

Off-white not pure white. Coral, not blue. Serif accents to break SaaS-monotony. We are a tool with personality.

4. Color = meaning.

Brand coral signals action. Semantic accents (green, amber, rose) signal state. Never decorative gradients.

5. Keyboard-first, mouse-fluent.

Every action has a shortcut. ⌘K opens the world. Hover states are bonuses, not requirements.

Motion

Short, calm, purposeful. Most transitions are 120–150ms. Never animate more than opacity, transform, and color.

120msHover state, sidebar items, chip color shift
150msButton press, tab switch, panel open
220msModal/drawer entrance, command palette
2s loopStatus dot pulse, "running" indicator
Easing: cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) for entrances · linear for indicators · ease-out for exits.

18 · Patterns

Voice & tone

Direct, technical, occasionally warm. We talk to engineers like equals.

Do
  • "47 rows · 0.023s" — facts, mono, no padding words
  • "Connection refused. Check your SSH tunnel." — point at the cause
  • "This query touches 12.4M rows. Run anyway?" — respect their time
  • "Good morning, Joel." — warmth in the right places
Don't
  • "Oops! Something went wrong 😅" — never cute about errors
  • "Unleash the power of your data!" — no marketing bombast
  • "Please wait while we process your request" — show progress, not apologies
  • "Awesome! Successfully connected." — say "Connected." Move on.
